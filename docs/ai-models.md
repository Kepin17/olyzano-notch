# AI models

*Settings → AI → AI models* is the one place for everything Notch downloads to work with audio, documents and pictures. It runs on this computer; nothing is downloaded until you press a button.

- **By category:** *Audio & recordings* (speech to text, high-quality speech, tell speakers apart, notes/decisions/tasks, music and sounds), *Documents & files* (find by meaning) and *Pictures* (read pictures, remove backgrounds, best-quality cut-out). Each row says what it is for, how big it is and how to use it.
- **Install together:** *Install recommended* gets everything each feature needs (about 6 GiB); *Install everything* adds the optional better-quality models (about 7 GiB). *Install all* in a category does that category only. A row's own button installs just that feature.
- **Shared programs:** the programs several features use (the language-model server, the speaker program, ONNX Runtime) are downloaded once, and *Remove* on a feature keeps a program another installed feature still needs.
- **While it downloads:** two files at a time, one progress bar for all, *Stop* keeps what is downloaded. A file that fails (no network, no room, a wrong checksum) is skipped and named when the rest is done; press the button again to retry. If there is not room for everything, nothing starts and the message says how much is needed.
- **Large models (graphics card):** for a computer with a good graphics card. *Best speech to text* (Whisper Large v3, about 3 GB), *Larger notes model* (Qwen3 8B, about 5 GB) and *Largest notes model* (Qwen3 14B, about 9 GB). They are never part of *Install recommended* or *Install everything*; each has its own *Download*. A model this computer cannot run (no Vulkan driver, no graphics card, an integrated processor that was not quicker in the speed test, or too little graphics memory: about 4, 7 and 11 GB) has its *Download* turned off and the reason under it.
- **Using them:** *Settings → AI → Recordings → Quality* gets *Best (graphics card)*, and *Notes, documents and Ask* chooses Standard, Larger or Largest. The one model writes the notes of recordings, summarizes documents (PDF) and answers in *Ask your files*. A larger model runs wholly on the graphics card; if it cannot start there (or the card is short of memory at that moment), Notch uses the standard model on the processor instead and says so in *Graphics card*.
- The graphics-card program (*Graphics card* below) and the text-recognition (OCR) language packs (*Languages*) keep their own places.
